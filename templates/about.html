About Bodim-Link NCP

Bodim-Link NCP is a centralized matchmaking platform designed specifically for university students in the Rajarata University area (Mihintale & Anuradhapura). Our mission is to provide a safe, reliable, and hassle-free way for students to find verified boarding places, and for property owners to easily manage their listings.
