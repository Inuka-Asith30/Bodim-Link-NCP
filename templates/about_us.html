About Bodim-Link NCP

Connecting University Students with Safe Boarding Places.

Our Mission

Bodim-Link NCP is a dedicated platform built for university students in the North Central Province (Anuradhapura & Mihinthale). Finding a safe, affordable, and reliable boarding place has always been a challenge for students coming from different parts of the country. We bridge the gap between students and verified boarding owners to make this process completely hassle-free.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted student accommodation platform in Sri Lanka.

100% Verified

All boarding owners are strictly verified using electricity bills to ensure student safety.

Easy Booking

Students can request visits online without unnecessary phone calls.
